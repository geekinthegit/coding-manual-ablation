=================================================

Source: TalkMoves coding manual PDF ("Talk Move Coding Manual for Public
Release"), Chapter 1 (pages 2-7). Chapter 2, table of contents,
references, and page numbers are excluded (3.1.1).

Transcription method
--------------------
Automatic extraction with pdftotext (poppler), pages 2-7, non-layout
mode, followed by rule-based correction and manual comparison against
the PDF. Symbols U+27A2 (arrow), U+25A0 (square), curly quotes, and the
ellipsis U+2026 were extracted as-is; no character substitution was
needed.

Line structure (rule B): one segmentation unit per line -- section
heading, move heading, paragraph, arrow item, square item, numbered
sub-item (1.1) -- with S:/T: speaker lines inside an example on
separate lines as in the PDF. No blank lines between units.

Definition of source fidelity used here: text content, symbols, item
order, and paragraph/item boundaries are preserved exactly. PDF layout
line-ends (line wraps produced by page width) are not preserved; they
are not part of the manual's structure. Source irregularities are kept
unchanged: "Teacher Talk Move Pressing for Reasoning" (no colon, 1.2),
"coded as Press for Reasoning" (1.5), "7/2 ?" (1.3), straight
apostrophes in "students' attention" (1.3) and "It's Company B because
that's" (1.5) alongside curly apostrophes elsewhere.

Verification of the transcription: after removing the five page-number
lines, the whitespace-split token sequence of the raw pdftotext output
is identical to that of manual/chapter1.txt (1382 tokens). Line
structure is the only difference.
